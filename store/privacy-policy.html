MediaConvert Privacy Policy

Publisher: Vinayak Benake
Effective date: 17 November 2026
Contact: Benakevinayak@gmail.com

Your media stays on your device

MediaConvert processes files you select on your Windows computer. The app has no media upload feature. It reads selected files and folders and saves converted output beside the source files. It does not operate an account system, advertising service, or analytics service.

Information used locally

File contents, names, folder paths, sizes, and conversion status are used to perform the conversion and show its progress. The conversion queue and file references are held in application memory. The app may store your saved conversion preferences, color theme and application cache locally. Converted files remain in your selected folders until you delete them.

Support correspondence

If you email the publisher, your email address and the information you choose to send are used to answer your request. Do not send sensitive files unless necessary and agreed with the publisher. Contact the email above to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any required retention.

Microsoft and Windows

Microsoft Store and Windows may process purchase, installation, or operating-system diagnostic information under their own policies. This policy describes MediaConvert's application behavior, not Microsoft's services.

Sharing and security

The publisher does not receive media through the app and does not sell app user data. Local files are protected by your device's account and security settings. The app does not add encryption to your original or converted files.

Children

The app has no accounts or data-collection features directed at children. Any support correspondence is handled as described above.

Changes

The publisher may update this policy when app behavior changes. The effective date will be revised. For questions about privacy, contact Benakevinayak@gmail.com.
